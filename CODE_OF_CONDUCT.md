# Code of Conduct

We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

Be considerate, patient, and constructive. Respect different backgrounds, nationalities, languages, abilities, and beliefs. Do not harass, discriminate against, threaten, or publish another person’s private information. Keep criticism focused on ideas and work.

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. To report an incident, contact the repository maintainers privately through GitHub.
